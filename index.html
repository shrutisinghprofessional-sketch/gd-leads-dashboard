How the numbers are worked out, and data checks
Source
From the sheet's utm_medium: Google_Paid → Google Search, video → Google Video, demandgen → Google Demand Gen, Facebook → Meta. Spelling variants (Incoming, incom, INcoming…) are merged.
Sale
Result is Sale Confirmed. Sale value is the SaleAmount on those rows.
Visit / booked
Visit Confirmed, Booked for Estimate, Estimated, For Payment, Rescheduled.
Follow-up pending
Will Get Back, Followup, Call Back, Will Call Self.
Not reached
No Answer, Busy, Not Connected, Voice Mail, Hung Up.
Lost
Rate Issue, Done from someone else / already booked elsewhere, Not Required, Cancelled, Refused by Operations, Wants Today Only / No Slots, Language Barrier.
Junk
Duplicate, Not Catering, Wrong Location, Only Enquiry, Haven't Enquired, Wrong Enquiry, Wrong Number.
Contact rate
Leads with any status other than Not reached, divided by leads that have a status.
Status change
A lead whose Result moved from one status to another, compared with its status at the start of that day. Several edits to one lead in a day count as one move.
Privacy
The dashboard holds counts only, no names or phone numbers.
